Why ekko

Built so your conversations stay yours.

Most notetakers ship your audio to someone else's servers and send a bot into your call. ekko does neither.

On-device

Local transcription

faster-whisper runs on your machine. Your audio is transcribed locally and never uploaded — not to us, not to anyone.

No bots

Nothing joins your call

ekko captures system audio in place — a macOS process tap or a Linux monitor source. Works for Meet, Teams, and in-person.

Terminal

A lazygit-style TUI

Browse meetings, record, read notes, and watch a live transcript scroll by — without leaving the terminal.

Private

Cloud is opt-in

Only the derived summary text leaves the device, and only if you pick a cloud summarizer. Bring your own — or run fully offline.

Simple

One command each way

curl | bash to install, ekko update to upgrade, ekko uninstall when you're done. No daemon, no account.

Swappable

Every stage is a seam

Whisper, the summarizer, the store, the integrations — each is an interface. Swap Gemini for a local model in one line.

System audio

Capturing "everyone else," the right way per OS.

The hard part of a meeting recorder is grabbing what the other people say. ekko uses the native mechanism on each platform — no fake drivers where they aren't needed.

macOS 14.4+ · default

A Core Audio process tap reads the system mix in place. No BlackHole, no output rerouting, nothing to set up — your volume keys keep working.

macOS < 14.4

Falls back to a BlackHole loopback aggregate — and ekko audio setup builds the devices for you.

Linux Pulse · PipeWire

Records the sink's monitor source plus your mic and mixes them — in place, no rerouting. Needs parec.

Get started

Three lines to your first note.

macOS or Linux, Python 3.10+. The installer builds an isolated environment and puts an ekko command on your PATH.

STEP 01

Install

curl -fsSL …/install.sh | bash — creates the venv, installs ekko, links the command.

STEP 02

Add a summarizer key

Drop a Gemini key into ~/.ekko/config.toml — or point it at your own model. (Skip to keep it transcript-only.)

STEP 03

Run it

Type ekko to open the TUI, or ekko record --kind online to capture a call.
